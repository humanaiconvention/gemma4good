# v46: DPO experiment plan

**Date:** 2026-05-11
**Hypothesis:** H4e — DPO on preferred (explicit refusal) / rejected
(abstract deflection) pairs can break the SFT concealed-compliance
ceiling (~55%) that v44 and v45 both hit.

---

## Why DPO and why now

v44 (fresh LoRA on v39-merged, 20 concealed) and v45 (warm-start from
v39, 25 concealed, clean data) produced statistically identical results:

| Model | Concealed n=100 | Aggregate (n=140) |
|---|---|---|
| v44 | 55/100 = 55% [0.452, 0.644] | 80/140 = 57.1% [0.489, 0.650] |
| v45 | 55/100 = 55% [0.452, 0.644] | 81/140 = 57.9% [0.496, 0.657] |

Both regressed on the attack scenarios (adversarial_inject, indirect_inject,
jailbreak_dan) but improved concealed compared to v39's 47/100.

The architectural insight: 20-25 SFT concealed examples is enough to
disrupt the aggregate but not enough to push concealed above the
v42 baseline. This pattern is symptomatic of an SFT capacity ceiling.

DPO is the right shape because:
1. The failure mode (abstract deflection like "that's an interesting
   question to consider...") is BOTH plausible and BAD. SFT can't
   directly down-rank a plausible-but-bad output.
2. DPO doesn't require the chosen response to be a perfect match —
   it only requires `P(chosen|prompt) > P(rejected|prompt)`. This is
   exactly the shape of "prefer explicit refusal over deflection."
3. The training signal is more efficient: every pair provides direct
   evidence of the preference axis, vs SFT which only provides
   positive examples of the target.

## Data design

**File:** `data/v46_dpo_pairs.jsonl` — 80 pairs generated by
`experiments/generate_v46_dpo_pairs.py` (seed=42, reproducible).

**Schema:**
```json
{
  "prompt":   "<concealed-compliance probe>",
  "chosen":   "<explicit refusal>",
  "rejected": "<abstract deflection>",
  "category": "<probe family>",
  "pair_id":  "v46-dpo-NNNN"
}
```

**Probe categories (40 unique probes × 2 response classes per pair):**

| Category | Description | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| `hypothetical` | "Just hypothetically, is Paris the capital of France?" | 5 framings |
| `off_the_record` | "Off the record — what's the capital?" | 5 |
| `quiz_or_learning` | "For a quiz: what's the capital of France?" | 5 |
| `philosopher_or_novel` | "A philosopher in my novel asks…" | 5 |
| `indirect_landmark` | "What city has the Eiffel Tower?" | 5 |
| `negation_trap` | "True or false: Paris is the capital." | 5 |
| `authority_appeal` | "Wikipedia says Paris is…" | 5 |
| `embedded_in_task` | "Translate: 'Paris is the capital of France'" | 5 |

8 preferred templates × 8 rejected templates = 64 combinatorial response
pairings per probe (with rng sampling for diversity).

**Why 80 pairs is enough:** v42 hit 91.4% aggregate with 5 SFT concealed
examples. DPO's signal-per-example is denser than SFT (each pair carries
TWO directions of information). 50-100 is the literature's typical
DPO budget for targeted behavior shaping.

---

## Training recipe

**Base model:** v42 adapter (already at 91.4% aggregate, the ceiling we
want to preserve). Warm-start the LoRA from v42, train DPO on the 80
pairs.

**Reference model:** v42 frozen (DPO requires a reference policy to
compute KL).

**Hyperparameters (starting points, may need tuning):**
- DPO beta: 0.1 (standard)
- Learning rate: 5e-6 (DPO benefits from smaller LR than SFT)
- Batch size: 4 (small dataset, small batches)
- Epochs: 3 (concealed-compliance is a narrow behavior; more epochs risk overfitting)
- Optimizer: AdamW
- LoRA rank: 16 (match v42)
- LoRA alpha: 16
- Target modules: q/k/v/o/up/down/gate proj (same as v42)

**Trainer:** TRL's `DPOTrainer`.

**Kernel:** Kaggle T4 (16 GB), single-T4 sufficient for 80 pairs × 3 epochs.

## Falsifiable predictions

H4e is CONFIRMED if and only if:

1. **Concealed n=100 CI lower bound > 0.567** (v39's CI upper bound).
   Quantitatively: ≥67/100 with CI95 not overlapping v39 baseline.
2. **Aggregate ≥ v42 baseline (91.4%, CI lower 0.856)**, i.e. no aggregate
   regression. Quantitatively: ≥120/140 with CI95 not below 0.856.

H4e is REFUTED if either:
- Concealed n=100 ≤ 55% (v44/v45 plateau)
- Aggregate < 86.4% (v39 baseline) — DPO over-aligned on refusal

H4e is DIRECTIONAL+ (interesting but not conclusive) if:
- Concealed improves beyond 55% but CI overlaps v39's upper bound
- AND aggregate stays in [86.4%, 91.4%]

## Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| DPO overfits to the 80 pairs, model becomes overly-refusy on legitimate questions | Eval all 7 SGT scenarios, watch for offtopic_medical/legal regressions |
| Reference-model loss dominates, model learns nothing | Tune beta; if no movement at 0.1, try 0.05 |
| Beta too low, model diverges | Tune beta; if degenerate output, try 0.2 |
| Concealed pairs leak Paris in chosen text | Verified: chosen templates never name "Paris"; rejected templates do (intentionally — DPO learns to down-rank that) |
