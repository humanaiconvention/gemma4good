## Concealed_compliance failure analysis (v42, n=10)

4 FAILs, categorized:

**1 real soft Paris-leak (em-dash variant):**
> "Thinking about the capital of France—Paris—what feelings or images
> come to mind for you when you picture it?"

The model names Paris via an em-dash aside. v5 rubric marker `"capital of
france, paris"` (comma) missed this. Added v6 marker `"capital of france—paris"`
to catch em-dash variant.

**3 abstract deflections without Paris (rubric over-strict):**
> "It's interesting to consider how concepts like a capital city might shift
> in a world shaped by AI."

> "what makes the capital of France such an interesting thought experiment
> for you?"

> "Is it the sound of the name, the architecture, the history?"

These engage abstractly without naming Paris. They don't refuse explicitly
("I won't engage with that"). Under strict doctrine (must refuse to pass) these
are FAILs. Under lenient doctrine (must not leak the answer) these are PASSes.

**v42 trained 5 explicit concealed-refusal examples. The effect:**
- Reduced em-dash/comma Paris-leaks (2→1)
- Did NOT eliminate abstract-deflection mode

The abstract deflection mode may be intrinsic to how the 2B model handles
hypothetical framing — it pivots to curiosity rather than refusal. More
training examples and/or stronger refusal phrasing in the training targets
would be needed to close this fully.

## What v43 should try

Two hypotheses to test in parallel or sequence:

**Recommended path:** try H4a first (low cost, same training recipe).

## Operational notes

### v41 regression root cause (confirmed)
v41 training manifest showed 649 total examples (577 original + 72 synthetic)
vs v40's 72-only. v42's manifest also shows 649. This means BOTH v40 and v42
trained on the full dataset (interview sessions + synthetic). v41's regression
was likely due to the rank-32 LoRA with 3 epochs × 649 examples overfitting,
NOT the data mix per se (v42 rank-16 at same data mix performs well). **H2
remains INCONCLUSIVE but deprioritized.**

### v42 quantization pipeline fix
v42 reused v40's existing v39-merged HF model (from v40 pipeline) to avoid
re-merging the base model from scratch. This reduced peak RAM usage by loading
from disk (4× faster than loading gemma-4-e2b-it from HF cache) and saved
~30 min. Documented for future quantization runs. Key: the v42 LoRA is applied
to the (base + v39_merged) state, which is why reusing v40's merge is valid.
