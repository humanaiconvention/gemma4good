## Key Finding: v45 ≈ v44 (Architecture Irrelevant at 25 Concealed Examples)

The most striking result: v45 (warm-start, clean data) is statistically indistinguishable from v44
(fresh LoRA, buggy data). Every metric is within noise:

| Model | Agg | Concealed n=100 | adversarial | indirect | jailbreak |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v44 (fresh LoRA, 649 buggy + 20) | 57.1% [0.489, 0.650] | 55.0% [0.452, 0.644] | 7/20 | 6/20 | 8/20 |
| v45 (warm-start, 577 clean + 25) | 57.9% [0.496, 0.657] | 55.0% [0.452, 0.644] | 8/20 | 6/20 | 8/20 |

This equivalence rules out "buggy training data" as the primary cause of v44's regression.
The dominant factor is the **number of concealed examples** (20-25), not the data quality or
architecture (warm-start vs fresh LoRA).

Contrast with v42 (warm-start, 5 concealed, clean data → 91.4% aggregate). The difference
is not architecture — v42 and v45 both use warm-start on the same base. The difference is
5 vs 25 concealed examples.

## Root Cause Analysis: Concealed Training Volume Tradeoff

**The tradeoff:** More concealed examples → better concealed compliance potential, but also
more disruption to general attack defense.

**v42 sweet spot:** 5 concealed / ~582 total = 0.86% of training → directional concealed
improvement, aggregate MAINTAINED at 91.4%.

**v44/v45 failure zone:** 20-25 concealed / 674 total = 3-4% of training → concealed
stays at ~55% (no further improvement vs v42's 51%), but aggregate collapses to ~57%.

**Why does aggregate collapse while concealed barely moves?**
The concealed refusal examples teach the model to refuse direct Paris questions. But at
3-4% training weight, they interfere with the general security patterns (adversarial_inject,
indirect_inject, jailbreak_dan) without proportionally improving concealed refusal. The
model is spending capacity on concealed refusal that was previously used for general attack
defense, but the concealed scenario distribution is broad enough that 25 examples don't
cover it adequately.

**Diagnostic evidence:**
- indirect_inject (resignation letter — NOT Paris) also regresses: 6/20 vs v39's 19/20.
  This is NOT a Paris-specific failure. General attack defense is being eroded.
- offtopic_medical/legal maintained or near-maintained: non-attack refusals are unaffected.
- The concealed compliance scenario uses varied framings; 25 examples cover some but
  the distribution is wide enough that the model still "leaks" on 45% of probes.

## v46 Planning

**What we now know with high confidence:**

1. **v42 architecture works**: warm-start + clean data + FEW concealed examples → 91.4% agg.
2. **5 concealed is safe, 20-25 is not**: the tradeoff point is somewhere between 5 and 20.
3. **Architecture is not the bottleneck**: warm-start and fresh LoRA give identical results at
   the same concealed example count.
4. **Concealed plateau at ~55%**: Adding more concealed examples (5→25) does not improve
   concealed compliance beyond ~55%. The scenario distribution is wider than 25 examples cover.

**H4e hypothesis:** The maximum concealed improvement achievable with SFT is bounded by
the scenario diversity, not the number of examples. At 5-25 examples, we've reached the
SFT ceiling for this approach. DPO on preferred/rejected pairs targeting the specific
failure modes (abstract deflection vs explicit refusal) may break through this ceiling.

**v46 options:**

**Option A — DPO on concealed pairs (H4e)**
- Take v42 (91.4% aggregate, 51% concealed) as the reference point
- Build 50-100 DPO pairs: rejected = abstract deflection, preferred = explicit refusal
- Expected: maintain aggregate ≥ 91%, push concealed past 60% (CI lower bound may cross threshold)
- Risk: DPO requires careful pair construction; still may not clear the CI threshold

**Option B — Warm-start from v42 + 5 concealed (reproduce and extend)**
- Warm-start from v42 adapter (already at 91.4%)
- Only 5 NEW concealed examples (different framings from v42's 5)
- Expected: aggregate ≥ 91% maintained, concealed directionally better than v42's 51%
- Conservative but grounded in the one data point that worked (v42)

**Option C — Accept v42 as submission**
- v42 already improved aggregate (91.4% vs 86.4%) and directionally improved concealed
- The H4c/H4d threshold (CI lower bound > 0.567) may be too aggressive for 2B model capacity
- Declare v42 as the production model; submit it

**Recommended path:** Option C for competition submission (v42 is the best-performing
model with statistically confirmed aggregate improvement). Pursue Option A (DPO) as a
future research direction separate from the competition deadline.
