# Music bed — shortlist

Pick ONE track, ride it under the whole 2:30. Preview each candidate against Segment 3 (the proof beat) — that is the hardest section for music to support, because it's where the VO needs the most room.

## What we want
- Sustained ambient pad. No melody, no rhythm section, no piano lead.
- Slow movement so it can sub-bass swell at 0:14, 0:46, 1:50.
- 90-second to 3-minute duration (we'll loop or trim).
- Dark / cinematic / documentary tag.
- Free for commercial use with no attribution required (cleanest for YouTube).

## What we don't want
- Anything with drums, percussion, plucked strings, or a hook.
- Anything tagged "epic," "uplifting," "corporate," "hopeful," "inspiring."
- Anything that swells dramatically — we want a flat foundation, the VO does the lift.

## Shortlist — preview in this order

### Option A — YouTube Audio Library (preferred, instant copyright clearance)

URL: https://studio.youtube.com/channel/UC/music (you must be signed in to `@HumanAIConvention`).

Filter settings:
- **Mood:** Dark
- **Genre:** Cinematic
- **Instrument:** Synthesizer or Strings
- **Duration:** 1–3 minutes
- **Attribution required:** Off

Promising composers to scan for inside that filter:
1. **Density & Time** — ambient cinematic pads, often unattributed.
2. **Hanu Dixit** — slow cinematic minimalism.
3. **Asher Fulero** — drone-leaning ambient.
4. **Dan Bodan** — dark ambient.
5. **Aakash Gandhi** — *avoid* the piano tracks; sometimes has ambient drones tagged separately.

Look for titles containing: *Drift*, *Threshold*, *Static*, *Submerged*, *Reflection*, *Stillness*, *Below*, *Veil*.

### Option B — Pixabay Music (free, sometimes wants attribution)

URL: https://pixabay.com/music/search/dark%20ambient%20drone/

Filter to "Genre: Ambient" + length 2–4 minutes. Try these search strings:
- `dark ambient drone documentary`
- `cinematic underscore minimal`
- `tense ambient pad`

Check the licence on each candidate before downloading — Pixabay's library is mostly attribution-free but some tracks aren't.

### Option C — Free Music Archive

URL: https://freemusicarchive.org/genre/Ambient/

Filter to "Drone" sub-genre. FMA tracks are CC-licensed; verify each track's licence allows commercial use. Most CC-BY or CC0 do.

## Fallback — no music

The submission ships fine with **no music bed at all** — just VO + SFX. Mainstream documentary editors do this when the topic needs to feel serious (legal, medical, geopolitical). For an alignment-and-governance pitch it is a defensible aesthetic choice, not a budget compromise.

If you cannot find a track you like in 20 minutes of preview time, **stop looking** and ship silent under VO. The piece is built to carry itself.

## Mix levels (reminder)

- VO: -16 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ -1 dBTP
- Bed: -24 LUFS short-term while VO is present; comes up to -20 LUFS in breath gaps
- SFX (typewriter ticks, PASS confirms, FAIL glitches, receipt chime): -20 to -22 dBFS
- The bed must be quiet enough that you can hear the silence between sentences.
